# 🌳 ThoughtTree — Zero-Clutter Keyboard-First Mind Mapping (v2.30)

**Live App:** [https://rogerwills61-source.github.io/thoughttree/](https://rogerwills61-source.github.io/thoughttree/)

**ThoughtTree** is a fast, smooth, distraction-free mind mapping app designed to get out of your way and let your ideas flow. Whether you are structuring complex plans at full keyboard speed on your laptop or capturing and organising ideas by touch on your phone, ThoughtTree gives you a calm, open canvas that saves automatically and stays in sync across your devices.

---

## Part 1: User Guide & Everyday Features

### 1. Clean, Clutter-Free Workspace & Smooth Navigation
- **Effortless Panning, Zooming & Fit to Screen:** Glide smoothly around even the largest mind maps using your mouse wheel, trackpad, or two-finger pinch-to-zoom on phones and tablets. Click **`Fit`** (or press **`Ctrl/Cmd + 0`**) at any time to centre and scale your entire map neatly inside your window, or click **`Tidy`** to sweep any manually moved topics back into a neat layout.
- **Simple 3-Part Top Bar:**
  - **Left:** Houses **`☰ Menu`**, **`⚙ Settings`**, and **`Undo` / `Redo`** (up to 60 steps back).
  - **Centre:** Shows your mind map title and a live status indicator (**`Auto-saved`**, **`Saved to file`**, or **`Synced live`**). Click the title to jump back to your Central Topic, or double-click to rename it.
  - **Right:** Quick controls to fold or expand levels of your map (`−`, `L1`, `L2`, `All`, `+`), **`Find`**, **`Fit`**, **`Tidy`**, and a **`?`** icon you can hover over on desktop for a quick keyboard cheat-sheet.
- **Focus Mode (`Alt + F`):** Hide the toolbars completely when you want zero distractions. A small **`Exit Zen Mode (Esc)`** button stays in the top-right corner so you can bring your toolbars back with a single click or keystroke—and your toolbars always return automatically the next time you open the app.
- **Overview Mini-Map:** When you zoom out on a large map, a handy mini-map appears automatically in the bottom-right corner to show where you are (you can set it to **Auto**, **Always**, or **Off** in Settings).

### 2. Fast Keyboard Capture on Desktop & Easy Touch Controls on Mobile
- **Keep Your Hands on the Keyboard (Desktop):**
  - **`Tab` / `Insert`:** Add a new branch underneath the selected topic and start typing straight away.
  - **`Enter` / `Shift + Enter`:** Add a new topic on the same level below (or above) the current one.
  - **`F2` or Just Start Typing:** Edit the selected topic directly on the canvas.
  - **`Arrow Keys` (`Shift + Arrows`):** Move smoothly around your map (or hold `Shift` to select multiple topics).
  - **`Alt + ↑` / `Alt + ↓`:** Move a topic up or down in the list of branches.
  - **`Space`:** Fold or expand the selected branch.
  - **`Ctrl/Cmd + C` / `X` / `V`:** Copy, cut, or paste entire branches—or paste bulleted lists straight from Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or plain text.
  - **`Alt + Drag` (or Drop onto Another Topic):** Move a branch and all its sub-topics under a different parent topic.
  - **`Shift + Drag` on Empty Space:** Draw a selection box to move or delete several topics at once.
  - **`Ctrl/Cmd + F` (`Find`):** Search all topic titles and notes, highlight matches on the map, and jump straight to any result (even if it is hidden inside a folded branch).
- **Designed for Phones & Tablets (Touch):**
  - **Bottom Action Bar:** On phones and smaller screens, a thumb-friendly bar at the bottom gives one-tap access to **`+ Child`**, **`+ Sibling`**, **`Edit`**, **`Note`**, **`Link`**, **`Fold`**, and **`Del`**.
  - **Double-Tap & Long-Press:** Double-tap any topic or connecting arrow to edit its text, or press and hold anywhere to open a full pop-up menu of actions that always fits neatly on screen.

### 3. Connect Related Ideas Across Branches (Cross-Links)
- **Draw Connecting Arrows (`L`):** Sometimes an idea on one side of your map relates to a topic on the other. Select any topic, press **`L`** (or tap **`Link`** on mobile), and click or tap the second topic to draw a curved dashed arrow between them.
- **Clean, Easy-to-Read Labels:** Click any dashed arrow to select it, then double-click or press **`F2`** / **`Enter`** to add a label. Long labels wrap neatly across lines and sit cleanly over the arrow without blocking your topics.
- **Automatic Tidying Around Topics:** Dashed arrows and their labels automatically curve around other topics and nearby arrows so your map stays readable even as it grows. Press **`Delete`** / **`Backspace`** while an arrow is selected to remove it.

### 4. Zoom In on Details or Step Back for the Big Picture (Layer Folding)
- **Step Through Levels Effortlessly:** Peel back or reveal your map one level at a time using **`[`** and **`]`** (or the **`−`** / **`+`** buttons in the top-right bar), or jump straight to Level 1 (**`L1`** / `Alt + 1`), Level 2 (**`L2`** / `Alt + 2`), or everything (**`All`** / `Alt + 0`).
- **Clear Branch Counts:** The top-right indicator shows how many levels are currently open (for example, `L2/4`), and any folded topic displays a small numbered badge showing how many topics are tucked inside.

### 5. Notes & Web Links on Any Topic (`N`)
- **Keep Extra Detail Handy Without Clutter:** Select any topic and press **`N`** (or tap **`Note`** / right-click **`Notes & Link...`**) to add detailed notes, reminders, or a clickable website link.
- **Pop-Up Preview Cards:** Topics with notes or links show a subtle coloured dot in the top-right corner. Hover briefly over the topic on desktop—or tap it on mobile—to read your notes or open the link from a clean pop-up card that never covers the topic itself.

### 6. Customise the Look & Feel (`⚙ Settings`)
Open **`⚙ Settings`** (or press **`Ctrl/Cmd + ,`**) to match ThoughtTree to your preference or screen:
- **3 Colour Themes:** Switch between **Dark**, **Light**, and **Sepia** (warm paper tone).
- **3 Line Styles:** Choose smooth **Curved** lines, neat **Square Elbow** lines, or **Straight** lines.
- **3 Map Layouts:** Grow your map **Balanced** (both left and right from the centre), **Right** only, or **Left** only.
- **3 Topic Shapes:** **Rounded**, **Pill**, or **Sharp** corners.
- **Colouring & Palettes:** Colour topics **By Layer** (each level gets its own colour), **By Branch**, or **Single Colour**, with four built-in palettes (**Ocean**, **Forest**, **Sunset**, and **Neon**).
- **Background Grid & Spacing:** Choose **Dots**, **Lines**, or **Off** for the background, and adjust map spacing between **Compact**, **Normal**, and **Spacious**.

### 7. Your Files, Your Way — Easy Saving, Sharing & Pasting
Your mind maps belong to you and are stored as simple, portable files:
- **Automatic Saving:** Every change is saved automatically on your device as you work. When you open or save a mind map file on a desktop browser (such as Chrome or Edge), ThoughtTree also saves your edits straight back into that file automatically—no need to keep hitting Save.
- **Flexible Import & Export:** Save and open standard **ThoughtTree (`.json`)** files, or import and export **Markdown outlines (`.md`)**, **FreeMind (`.mm`)** maps, and **PNG images (`.png`)** for reports and presentations.
- **Paste Straight from Word & Google Docs:** Copy a bulleted or numbered list from Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or a text document, click a topic in ThoughtTree, and press **`Ctrl/Cmd + V`**—your entire indented list turns into structured mind map branches automatically.

### 8. Automatic Live Sync Across Your Devices
- **Work Seamlessly Between Laptop, Tablet & Phone:** Save a mind map file (for example, into your Google Drive folder) and open that same file on your phone or tablet. Both screens link up automatically in the background—no accounts, logins, or room codes needed.
- **Fast, Private & Self-Healing:** Edits, drags, notes, and cross-links appear on your other screen almost instantly, and the connection automatically heals itself if your phone goes to sleep, switches Wi-Fi, or returns from a file picker.
- **Safe Branching with `Save As`:** If you save a copy of a map under a new name using **`Save As...`**, the new copy gets its own completely separate sync channel so changes never bleed back into the original file.

### 9. Install on Laptop, Tablet & Mobile (App Mode)
You can install ThoughtTree directly onto your device so it opens in its own full-screen window with its own tree icon—and works even when you are offline:
- **💻 Laptop / Desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux, Chromebook):**
  1. Open [https://rogerwills61-source.github.io/thoughttree/](https://rogerwills61-source.github.io/thoughttree/) in **Chrome**, **Edge**, or **Brave**.
  2. Click **`☰ Menu`** (top-left) → **`📲 Install ThoughtTree App`** *(or click the **Install** icon on the right side of your browser's address bar)*.
- **📱 Android Phone & Tablet:**
  1. Open the live link in **Chrome for Android**.
  2. Tap **`☰ Menu`** (top-left) → **`📲 Install ThoughtTree App`** *(or tap Chrome's `⋮` menu → **Install app**)*.
- **🍎 iPhone & iPad (iOS / iPadOS):**
  1. Open the live link in **Safari**.
  2. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom of Safari and select **Add to Home Screen**.

## Part 2: Technical Architecture & Engineering Deep-Dive

For developers and architects, **ThoughtTree** is built as a zero-dependency, single-file HTML5 Canvas Progressive Web App (`index.html`) governed by a persistent Architecture Decision Record (ADR) discipline (`ADR-001` through `ADR-041`).

### 1. Core Performance & UI Architecture Invariants
- **60 FPS Viewport-Culled Canvas Renderer (`ADR-002` & `ADR-012`):** All connectors, nodes, collapsed badges, cross-links, and marquee selections render on a DPR-scaled HTML5 `<canvas>` via `requestAnimationFrame` with bounding-box viewport culling (`pad = 220px`), sustaining smooth 60 FPS pan/zoom/drag even at 1,000+ nodes.
- **Ephemeral Inline DOM Editor & Debounced ARIA Mirror (`ADR-002`, `ADR-005` & `ADR-039`):** A single transient `<input id="nodeEditor">` overlays the active node or cross-link anchor only while editing, preserving native OS spellcheck, selection, and mobile virtual keyboards without DOM bloat. A visually hidden `role="tree"` ARIA mirror (`#ariaTree`) syncs off the critical render path via a `180ms` debounce.
- **Universal Viewport-Boundary Clamping (`ADR-033` & `ADR-034`):** Every floating overlay (`#contextMenu`, `#nodeCallout`, `#helpPopover`, `#fileMenu`, `#settingsPanel`, `#searchModal`, `#noteModal`, `#guideModal`) measures against `window.visualViewport` after an off-frame `(left: 0, top: 0)` coordinate reset, flips direction automatically around screen edges, respects top HUD (`62px`) and mobile dock (`72px`) safe margins, and enforces `max-height: calc(100dvh - 24px); overflow-y: auto`.

### 2. Normalized Flat Store & Width-Aware $O(N)$ Layout Engine
- **Single Source of Truth (`ADR-001` & `ADR-037`):** Mind maps are stored as a normalized flat dictionary (`mapId`, `fileSyncToken`, `rootId`, `nodesById`, `linksById`, `deletedById`) rather than nested trees, enabling $O(1)$ node lookups, clean 60-step immutable Undo/Redo snapshots, and deterministic CRDT merging.
- **Edge-to-Edge Width-Aware Layout (`ADR-016`):** `computeLayout()` calculates subtree heights in $O(N)$ and positions children from pill edge to pill edge (`parentHalfW + gap + childHalfW`) with dedicated Level-1 separation (`rootEdgeGap`, `l1BranchGap`) around the Central Topic across **Balanced**, **Right**, and **Left** directions.
- **Pre-Calculated Layout & Cached Bounds Fast-Paths (`ADR-039`–`ADR-041`):**
  - `hydrateAllNodeBounds()` reuses stored `metadata.width` and `metadata.height` values on load, bypassing redundant synchronous `ctx.measureText()` loops on mobile CPUs.
  - `loadFileContent()` checks for unpositioned non-root nodes (`n.id !== imported.rootId`) and skips redundant $O(N)$ `applyAutoLayout()` passes on saved `.json` files, painting the opened map on screen (`fitToScreen()` + `drawFrame()`) in $< 16\text{ms}$ before deferring IndexedDB persistence and sync channel binding to a background tick.

### 3. Joint Multi-Pass Global Cross-Link Solver (`ADR-027`–`ADR-029`)
- **System-Wide Obstacle Avoidance (`resolveAllCrossLinksLayout`):** Rather than routing cross-links in isolation, the solver evaluates **14 perpendicular curvature multipliers** ($\pm 0.85\text{x}$ to $\pm 5.0\text{x}$) $\times$ **5 label anchor `t`-slots** (`0.5, 0.34, 0.66, 0.24, 0.76`) per link against:
  1. All visible node pills ($28\text{px} \times 22\text{px}$ label clearance + 5-point curve sampling).
  2. All other cross-link multi-line label blocks ($26\text{px} \times 18\text{px}$ label-to-label buffer).
  3. All other cross-link curves (preventing label-on-curve overlap and enforcing $\ge 28\text{px}$ parallel lane separation).
- **3-Pass Coordinate-Descent Relaxation:** Runs up to 3 global relaxation passes so existing links smoothly shift to adjacent arcs or label slots whenever a new nearby cross-link is added.
- **Borderless Knockout Typography (`measureWrappedLinkLabel`):** Greedily word-wraps link annotations at `150px` max width (`16px` line height) and renders them with a `7px` `canvasBg` halo stroke (`strokeText`) followed by crisp fill text.

### 4. Self-Healing Cryptographic File-Bound Sync Engine (`ADR-037`–`ADR-041`)
- **Zero-Backdoor Cryptographic Channel Derivation:**
  - Every map carries a `mapId` and a **256-bit `fileSyncToken`** (32 random bytes / 64 hex chars via `crypto.getRandomValues`).
  - Channel keys derive strictly via `getPrivateFileChannelKey()` (`tt_v26_<mapId>_<tokenPrefix>`)—**never** from document titles or filenames.
  - **Identity Lifecycle:** Normal `Save` (`Ctrl + S`) preserves `mapId` and `fileSyncToken`; `New Blank Map`, `Save As...` (`Ctrl + Shift + S`), and demo generation rotate both (`rotateMapIdentityForBranch()`) to isolate branched files.
  - **Deterministic Legacy Migration (`ADR-038`):** Older `.json` files lacking `fileSyncToken` derive a deterministic 64-hex-char migration key (`deriveLegacyMigrationToken`) from their internal node UUID structure + `rootId`, hydrate missing per-node `updatedAt` timestamps, and auto-flush the upgraded token to disk on desktop.
- **Tri-Layer Self-Healing Transport ($< 50\text{ms}$ Latency, Zero External JS Libraries):**
  1. **Layer 1 — Same-Browser Tabs (`BroadcastChannel`):** Instant `0ms` sync across tabs bound to `activeChannelKey`.
  2. **Layer 2 — Multi-Broker Raw Binary MQTT v3.1.1 over WebSockets:** Connects concurrently to `broker.emqx.io`, `broker.hivemq.com`, and `test.mosquitto.org` using custom binary encoders (`CONNECT 0x10`, `SUBSCRIBE 0x82`, `PUBLISH 0x30/0x31`, 20s `PINGREQ 0xc0`), a streaming multi-byte Variable Byte Integer frame decoder (`parseIncomingMqttFrames`) for large snapshots, `CONNACK` retained `sync-hello` publishing, and **hot-switched topic subscriptions** (`ADR-039`) that keep TLS sockets open across file switches.
  3. **Layer 3 — Direct Peer-to-Peer WebRTC DataChannels (`RTCPeerConnection`):** Signals `webrtc-offer`, `webrtc-answer`, and `webrtc-ice` (`stun:stun.l.google.com:19302`) over the private MQTT topic and upgrades the status pill to **`⚡ P2P Live · HH:MM`** once open.
- **Conflict-Free LWW-Element-Set CRDT Merge & Wake Guard:**
  - `mergeRemoteCRDTState()` enforces an unconditional security guard (`remote.mapId === local.mapId && remote.fileSyncToken === local.fileSyncToken`), merges `deletedById` tombstones via `Math.max` to prevent zombie nodes/links, locks actively edited (`editingId`, `editingLinkId`) or dragged (`draggedNodeId`) items, and strictly isolates local camera `viewport` (`offsetX`, `offsetY`, `scale`).
  - `visibilitychange`, `online`, and `focus` listeners trigger `healSyncConnectionsOnWake()`, protected by an `isOpeningFilePicker` + `1500ms` suppression guard (`ADR-041`) so returning from Android's file picker never broadcasts stale map state over an incoming file load.

### 5. Local-First Persistence & Mobile PWA Ergonomics
- **Singleton `IndexedDB` & Zero-Prompt Session Handles (`ADR-032` & `ADR-039`):** Reuses a cached singleton `IndexedDB` connection (`_cachedDbPromise`) for instant local saves and keeps desktop `FileSystemFileHandle` strictly in-memory so `file:///` and PWA reloads never trigger permission prompts.
- **Android Non-Media Document Picker (`ADR-041`):** Uses `<input type="file" accept="application/json,text/plain,text/xml,.json,.md,.mm">` on touch/mobile viewports so Android Chrome skips the Camera/Video media chooser and routes directly to **Files / Google Drive**.
- **Deferred PNG Icon Rasterisation & Cache-First SW (`ADR-036` & `ADR-038`):** Attaches the SVG ThoughtTree emblem immediately and defers $192 \times 192$ / $512 \times 512$ PNG rasterisation to `requestIdleCallback` alongside a Cache-First (`Stale-While-Revalidate`) Service Worker (`thoughttree-pwa-v30`).
