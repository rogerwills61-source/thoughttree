# 🌳 ThoughtTree — Zero-Clutter Keyboard-First Mind Mapping

**ThoughtTree** is a fast, distraction-free, 60 FPS mind mapping Progressive Web App (PWA) designed for instant keyboard capture on desktop and effortless thumb-zone navigation on mobile.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Keyboard-First Speed:** Press `Tab` to create a child node, `Enter` for a sibling node, `F2` (or just start typing) to edit text, `Space` to fold/expand branches, and `Alt + Up/Down` to reorder siblings.
- **Non-Hierarchical Dashed Cross-Links (`L`):** Connect any two nodes with curved dashed relationship arrows and multi-line borderless halo labels that automatically route around obstacles.
- **Progressive Layer Folding (`[` / `]`):** Step inward or outward layer-by-layer or jump directly to `L1`, `L2`, or `All`.
- **Notes & Web Links (`N`):** Attach detailed notes and clickable URLs to any node with a non-occluding hover/tap callout card.
- **100% Local-First & Live Cross-Device Sync:** Auto-saves continuously to local `IndexedDB` and syncs live across your open devices via conflict-free per-node CRDTs.
- **Zero Lock-In Import/Export:** Full round-trip support for `.json`, indented Markdown outlines (`.md`), FreeMind XML (`.mm`), Microsoft Word/Google Docs nested lists (`Ctrl/Cmd + V`), and `.png` images.

## 📲 How to Install ThoughtTree Across Your Devices

### 💻 Laptop / Desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux, Chromebook)
1. Open your hosted ThoughtTree `https://` URL in **Chrome**, **Edge**, or **Brave**.
2. Click **`☰ Menu`** (top-left) → **`📲 Install ThoughtTree App`** *(or click the **Install** icon on the right side of your browser's address bar)*.
3. ThoughtTree launches in its own standalone window and pins to your Taskbar, Start Menu, or Dock—working 100% offline.

### 📱 Android Phone & Tablet
1. Open your hosted ThoughtTree `https://` URL in **Chrome for Android**.
2. Tap **`☰ Menu`** (top-left) → **`📲 Install ThoughtTree App`** *(or tap Chrome's three-dot menu `⋮` → **Install app**)*.
3. ThoughtTree installs directly into your Android app drawer with its custom tree icon and full-screen touch interface.

### 🍎 iPhone & iPad (iOS / iPadOS)
1. Open your hosted ThoughtTree `https://` URL in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with upward arrow) at the bottom of Safari and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
